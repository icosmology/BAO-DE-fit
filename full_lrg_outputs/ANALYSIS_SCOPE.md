Full DR1 v1.5 NGC+SGC data and complete random_0. No object thinning. Pre-reconstruction clustering, exact moment-based first and second redshift derivative estimators, and a joint physical-template BAO fit with free distance anchors. Jackknife covariance, actual RR redshift windows, finite redshift basis, broad Gaussian amplitude priors, and damping priors are used. This is not an official DESI analysis or a mock-validated publication likelihood. See fit summaries for convergence, basis/covariance sensitivity and any prior-boundary effects. Raw clustering measurements are distinct from model-conditional distance derivatives.
